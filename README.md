# ⚡ CYBERPULSE: OVERCLOCK

An adrenaline-pumping 2D Sci-Fi / Cyberpunk Action Roguelite inspired by **Hades** and **Dead Cells**, built with TypeScript, HTML5 Canvas, and Web Audio API synthesis.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

### 1. Launch Options
- **Live Local Server**: Open [http://localhost:5173/](http://localhost:5173/) in your web browser.
- **Standalone Offline Play**: Simply double-click [`play_standalone.html`](play_standalone.html) in your browser!

### 2. Controls
- **WASD / Arrow Keys**: Move operative
- **Space / Shift**: Phase Dash (invulnerability frames & trail effects)
- **Left Click**: 3-Hit Katana Combo
  - *Slash 1*: Quick horizontal blade sweep (35 dmg)
  - *Slash 2*: Upward cleave (45 dmg)
  - *Slash 3*: 360° Whirlwind Finisher (75 dmg + heavy knockback + screen shake)
- **Right Click / Q**: Laser Sidearm / Plasma Cannon
- **E**: Activate **OVERCLOCK** (when gauge is full)
  - Slows enemy time by ~55%
  - Increases player move speed by +40%
  - 100% Critical Strike Chance on all attacks!
- **Gamepad Supported**: Full native support for Xbox / PlayStation / standard USB controllers!

## 🧬 Cyberware Implants & Synergies
After clearing each combat arena, choose from Hades-style holographic reward gates:
- **⚡ Photon Edge Emitter**: +35% sword range and deflects enemy lasers back into hostiles!
- **🔋 Tesla Surge Actuator**: Dashing discharges chain lightning across up to 4 targets.
- **🩸 Nanite Bio-Siphon**: Restores health on melee hits and critical strikes.
- **🔥 Plasma Overdrive Core**: Ignites slashed foes with continuous burn damage.
- **👻 Ghost Protocol Matrix**: +1 Dash charge and +25% dash distance.
- **🛸 Autonomous Assault Drone**: Escort drone that auto-fires homing micro-lasers.
- **💠 Quantum Hyper-Capacitor**: Emits an EMP blast stunning all enemies upon Overclocking.
- **🎯 Synaptic Precision Chip**: Massive critical hit damage multiplier.
- **🛡️ Titanium Exoskeleton**: +50 Max HP and instant nano-repair.
- **⚡ Adrenal Overdrive**: Defeating enemies while Overclocked extends its duration!

## 🏛️ Neural Lab (Meta Progression)
Collect **Meta Cores 💠** during runs to permanently augment your operative in the Neural Lab:
1. **Reinforced Hull Plating**: +Starting Max HP
2. **Neural Accelerator**: +Movement Speed
3. **High-Frequency Blade**: +Base Katana Damage
4. **Capacitor Overcharge**: +Overclock Duration
5. **Encrypted Nanite Stash**: +Starting Credits

## 👾 Enemy Hostiles & Boss
- **Scout Drone**: Floating ranged unit that strafes and fires pulse lasers.
- **Neon Stalker**: Fast assassin that lunges with twin plasma daggers.
- **Cyber Enforcer**: Armored heavy unit that smashes the ground to emit shockwave rings.
- **Sniper Unit**: Long-range assassin telegraphing deadly railgun shots.
- **Sector Boss: "PROJECT TITAN"**: Multi-phase heavy mech featuring spread salvos, homing missiles, and rage ground-pound phases.
